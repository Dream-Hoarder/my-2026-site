// 01 — About

Architect of Systems.
Builder of Worlds.

I'm Willie Bonner Jr., a Technical Architect and Systems Security Specialist based in Houston, TX. I operate under a Zero-Trust paradigm — every environment is hostile by default, and every system I deploy reflects that discipline.

My foundation spans hardened operating systems including Qubes OS, Kodachi, Fedora, Ubuntu, and Arch Linux, with deep expertise in kernel-level hardening, LUKS encryption, qrexec policy management, and network obscurity via Proton VPN, Quad9, and custom proxy architectures.

Professionally, I fund technical execution through expertise in Healthcare Revenue Cycle — high-volume medical billing, insurance eligibility verification, and collections optimization. The operational discipline from that domain feeds directly into how I engineer systems.

My long game is scaling Pixel Prodigy Studios into a premier global entity — synthesizing human intent with autonomous AI to engineer seamless entertainment platforms backed by battle-hardened security infrastructure.
